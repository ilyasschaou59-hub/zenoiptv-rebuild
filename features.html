Features

A straightforward overview of how ZenoIPTV plans work, so you know what to expect before you order.

What every plan gives you

A choice of device options

Plans are available for 1, 2, 3 or 4 devices, so you can match your subscription to how you watch.

Four subscription lengths

Subscribe for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months or 12 months, paid in one payment for the period you choose.

Lower monthly cost on longer plans

For every device option, the 3-, 6- and 12-month plans cost less per month than paying monthly. The pricing page shows the exact figures.

Use on common device types

IPTV is typically watched through a compatible player app on a smart TV, streaming device, phone, tablet or computer. See compatibility for details.

Direct WhatsApp support

Ask questions about plans, ordering or setup by messaging our team on WhatsApp.

Help before you buy

Not sure which plan suits you? Send us your device details and we'll help you choose before you order.

Choosing the right plan

Two questions decide which plan fits you.

How many devices?

Count the devices you plan to use with your subscription and choose the plan for that number, from 1 to 4.

How long?

Monthly plans offer the most flexibility. Longer plans reduce the average monthly cost.

Before you order: check that your device is compatible. If you have any doubts, message our team on WhatsApp with your device make and model.
