Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Email support@zenoiptv.com.

Plans and pricing

ZenoIPTV offers subscription plans for 1, 2, 3 or 4 devices. Each plan is available for 1 month, 3 months, 6 months or 12 months. You can compare every option on the pricing page.

Each plan includes a set number of devices, from 1 to 4. Choose the plan that matches the number of devices you plan to use. If you are unsure which plan you need, email support@zenoiptv.com before ordering.

Yes. For every device option, the 3-, 6- and 12-month plans cost less per month than paying monthly. The pricing page shows the monthly equivalent and the difference for each plan.

All prices are shown in euros (EUR).

Prices may be updated over time. The price that applies to your order is the one confirmed with you before you pay.

Ordering and payment

Choose a plan on the pricing page and select Order. This opens the contact page with your plan already selected, so you can email our support team. Support replies with the next steps.

Available payment options are confirmed by support when you place your order. If you'd like to check before ordering, email support@zenoiptv.com.

If you would like to change the number of devices or your subscription length, contact support to discuss the options available.

Please read our terms of service. If you have any questions about refunds, contact support before ordering.

Devices and setup

See the compatibility page for the device types people commonly use. Compatibility depends on your device and the player app you use. Email support with your device make and model to confirm before you order.

IPTV services are usually watched through a compatible player app on your device. Support can tell you which setup suits your device.

Streaming quality depends on your internet connection and home network. If you have questions about your connection, contact support before ordering.

Support

Email support@zenoiptv.com or use the form on the contact page. The form opens your own email app with a pre-filled message.

Include the plan you are interested in, your device make and model, and a short description of your question. This helps support answer you more quickly.

Still have a question?

Our support team can help with plans, ordering and device setup.
