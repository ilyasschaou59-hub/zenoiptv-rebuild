Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This privacy policy explains what personal information ZenoIPTV ("we", "us") handles when you visit zenoiptv.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Who we are

This website is operated by ZenoIPTV. For privacy questions or requests, contact us on WhatsApp.

Information we collect

When you browse this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the website running.

When you contact us

This website does not provide a contact form or collect contact details. If you contact us on WhatsApp, the information you choose to share is handled by WhatsApp and received by ZenoIPTV so we can respond to your request.

When you order

If you order a subscription, we process the information needed to set up and manage your subscription and to communicate with you about it.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your questions and support requests
  • To process and manage your order and subscription
  • To keep this website secure and working properly
  • To meet legal obligations that apply to us

We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing your information

We share information only with service providers who help us run our business, such as our website hosting provider, and only as needed for them to provide their services. WhatsApp is a third-party service with its own privacy practices. We may also disclose information where required by law.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purposes described above, or as required by law.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, contact us on WhatsApp.

Third-party websites and apps

This website may link to other websites, and you may use third-party player apps on your devices. Those websites and apps have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us on WhatsApp.
