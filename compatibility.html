Device compatibility

IPTV is usually watched through a player app on your device. Here are the device types people commonly use, and how to confirm yours before ordering.

Common device types

Support for a specific device depends on its model, operating system version and the player apps available for it.

  • Smart TVs

    Many smart TVs can run IPTV player apps from their built-in app store, depending on the brand, model and operating system.

  • Android TV and Google TV

    TVs and set-top boxes running Android TV or Google TV can generally install player apps from the Google Play Store.

  • Amazon Fire TV

    Fire TV Stick and other Fire TV devices can run player apps available for Fire OS.

  • Apple TV, iPhone and iPad

    Apple devices can use player apps available on the App Store.

  • Android phones and tablets

    Android phones and tablets can use player apps from the Google Play Store.

  • Computers

    Windows and macOS computers can use desktop media player apps that support IPTV.

Please confirm before you order. This list is general guidance, not a guarantee that a particular device will work. Message our team on WhatsApp with your device make and model and we'll let you know.

What to check

  1. Your device

    Find the make, model and operating system version in your device settings.

  2. Your connection

    Streaming depends on a stable internet connection and home network.

  3. Number of devices

    Choose a plan that covers every device you plan to use, from 1 to 4.

  4. Ask us on WhatsApp

    If you're unsure, message our team on WhatsApp before ordering.
